# Style guide — The Lighthouse

- Close third person, always Nell's point of view. Past tense.
- Plain, concrete words. Prefer the sea, the stone, the weather to abstractions.
- Short to medium sentences; one long sentence per paragraph at most.
- No adverbs on dialogue tags: "he said", "she said", nothing fancier.
- Do not name emotions; show them through the body or an object.
- No more than one simile per paragraph.
- Dialogue is sparse. People in Carrow say less than they mean.
- End scenes on an image, not on an explanation.
- British spelling.
