# Character sheet — template

*One sheet per main or important character. It belongs in the novel's bible, next to the
manuscript, not in the handbook. The § references point to [`handbook.md`](handbook.md).*

*Fill it in as you go, not all at once. An empty box is an open question, not a fault. What
gets decided while writing (an age, a scar, a word) goes in the same day, or it will be
contradicted in chapter 30.*

## Under pressure

*A light to see by, never a box to fit. A personality type says where the character starts
from when things get tense, not what they will do: the contradictions on this sheet always
win over the type.*

- **Personality type** (for example in Taibi Kahler's Process Communication model), if the
  author finds it useful: base, and phase if it differs.
- **First sign of strain**: what they still do politely, but too much.
- **As it rises**: the mask, what they do to others or to themselves.
- **At rock bottom**: what they then believe about themselves or the world.
- **What restores them**: what they need to come back.
