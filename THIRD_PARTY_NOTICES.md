## Craft rules

`atelier/mod/skills/craft-rules/craft-rules.md` restates, in original wording, writing
advice credited to published authors and practitioners. Its before/after examples are
translated from the secondary sources that presented the rules (TikTok carousels by
@senswriter, texts by Nikoleta Ivanova and Midnight Editors) and are credited under each
rule; its "Sources" section lists where each rule was found.
